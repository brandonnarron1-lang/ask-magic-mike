# Ask Magic Mike — Thirty-Day Executive Report Source

This is a blank evidence-driven source, not a claim of future results.

1. What entered — live leads and test exclusions.
2. Where it came from — first/last touch, page, placement, campaign, form.
3. Lead quality — score/grade distribution with deterministic factors.
4. Response speed — assignment and first-contact distributions.
5. Appointments and nurture — measured outcomes only.
6. Signed clients and closings — measured outcomes only.
7. Attributed revenue — recognized values with source notes.
8. Operational failures — delivery, queue, duplicate, SLA, auth, and form issues.
9. Form performance — Form 3 plus any individually approved activation.
10. Owned-traffic learning — links, QR scans, content, and conversion.
11. Investment decision — keep paid validation inactive, begin a separately
    approved limited test, or reconsider after correcting evidence gaps.

Every chart must distinguish zero from not yet measured and exclude QA records.
