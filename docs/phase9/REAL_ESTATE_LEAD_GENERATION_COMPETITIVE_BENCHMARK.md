# Ask Magic Mike Phase 9 competitive benchmark

Reviewed: 2026-08-18  
Scope: United States residential real-estate lead generation, portal programs, IDX/CRM platforms, paid media, predictive seller systems, database nurture, AI engagement, and Tom Ferry operating systems.

## Executive conclusion

The market does not contain one unbeatable lead-generation product. It contains several specialized operating models:

1. **Portals rent active consumer demand.** Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, and Redfin concentrate shoppers, then sell placement, introductions, live transfers, touring requests, or referral-fee relationships.
2. **All-in-one platforms manufacture and manage demand.** CINC, BoomTown, BoldTrail/kvCORE, Sierra Interactive, Ylopo, Lofty, Brivity, Real Geeks, and Luxury Presence combine IDX, paid media, behavioral data, CRM, automation, and AI nurture.
3. **Operational CRMs enforce response and follow-up.** Follow Up Boss is the clearest benchmark for lead-source ingestion, distribution rules, action plans, team inboxes, communication history, and agent accountability.
4. **Predictive and homeowner systems mine the database.** Fello, SmartZip, Offrs, Revaluate, RealScout, Homebot, and similar systems enrich homeowner records, rank likely movers, monitor engagement, and trigger valuation or market conversations.
5. **Advertising platforms optimize only when outcomes return.** Meta, Google, and TikTok improve when the brokerage sends clean first-party conversion events, offline outcomes, and CRM-stage feedback.
6. **Coaching systems turn tools into production.** Tom Ferry's model repeatedly centers the database, geographic farming, social authority, open houses, local market content, referral cadence, time-blocked prospecting, and weekly accountability.

The winning Ask Magic Mike strategy is not to imitate every interface. It is to become the **brokerage-owned intelligence and orchestration layer** that connects all useful channels while retaining first-party identity, local context, consent, attribution, operational history, economics, and outcomes.

## Competitive archetypes

### 1. Portal demand systems

| System | What makes it work | Operating advantage | Structural weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zillow Premier Agent / Flex | ZIP- or market-based exposure, share of voice, active-shopper connections, touring requests, live acceptance windows, ISA and buyer-agent specialization, performance requirements | High consumer traffic and strong intent moments | Rented audience, market-dependent cost, possible shared competition, platform rules, weak first-party moat unless the brokerage captures the relationship quickly |
| Realtor.com Connections Plus / ReadyConnect Concierge | Pre-screened inquiries, automated follow-up, live-transfer or claim workflows, CRM/app delivery, performance coaching, upfront or referral economics depending on program | Strong portal intent and concierge qualification | Cost or referral share, dependence on platform inventory, source economics must be reconciled through close |
| Homes.com Membership / Boost | Listing priority, listing-agent branding, retargeting, neighborhood and school visibility, listing analytics, direct lead positioning | Strong listing presentation and brand ownership narrative | Paid visibility still requires conversion operations and local differentiation |
| Redfin Partner | Motivated referrals, no upfront inventory cost in referral model, fee at close, performance-based allocation | Low upfront acquisition risk | Margin sharing and dependence on external referral supply |

**Ask Magic Mike response:** accept portal demand as a measurable channel, never as the system of record. Every portal lead must enter one normalized contract, carry first- and last-touch attribution, hit an SLA, receive a clear owner, and report qualification, appointment, agreement, close, revenue, and referral cost.

### 2. All-in-one IDX, CRM, and advertising platforms

| System | Distinctive mechanisms | Useful pattern to adopt |
|---|---|---|
| CINC | Hyperlocal IDX, Google and Meta advertising, behavioral intelligence, AutoTracks, AI nurture, dialer, seller suite, current FUB integration | Connect site behavior, property alerts, nurture, and handoff rather than treating form submission as the only intent signal |
| BoomTown | IDX, predictive CRM, lead generation, NOW mobile app, Success Assurance database monitoring and long-horizon nurture | First-hour response plus year-long recycle of the whole database |
| BoldTrail / kvCORE | Large lead-generation toolkit, IDX, behavior automation, property alerts, campaigns, dialer, reporting, listing promotion, Vitals dashboard | One operator surface connecting acquisition, action, accountability, and listing promotion |
| Sierra Interactive | SEO/AEO-oriented IDX, integrated CRM, lead routing, nurture, ad management | Build owned search authority and high-converting local pages instead of depending entirely on paid portals |
| Ylopo | PPC, seller valuation, dynamic social, LSA, behavioral listing alerts, remarketing, priority scores, AI and voice nurture, database revival | Personalize property and market follow-up from observed behavior while preserving consent and human takeover |
| Lofty | Agentic AI operating system, CRM, IDX, Smart Plans, mobile, ads, AI appointment setting | Separate low-risk autonomous observation and drafting from high-risk execution authority |
| Brivity | CRM, IDX, automation, transactions, phone attribution, AI call summaries, daily opportunity data products | Track calls and offline activity as first-class attribution and intent events |
| Real Geeks | Hyperlocal websites, CRM, mobile push, property activity, valuation, AI, branded consumer app | Keep the brokerage brand in the consumer's daily property-search loop |
| Luxury Presence | Premium sites, SEO/GEO, paid media, AI CRM, property and homeowner alerts, AI nurture, collaborative search, CMA and app experiences | Premium brand, owner-controlled data, short response time, long nurture, and human quality oversight can coexist |

**Ask Magic Mike response:** remain modular. Integrate useful acquisition and CRM providers, but keep canonical lead identity, source truth, permission decisions, routing, outcomes, experiments, and intelligence inside the brokerage-owned platform.

### 3. CRM and team execution systems

Follow Up Boss is the operational benchmark because its value is not a flashy landing page. Its durable mechanisms are:

- broad lead-source ingestion;
- source-specific lead-flow rules;
- round robin and first-to-claim distribution;
- action plans with tasks, emails, texts, and long nurture;
- team inbox, tracking numbers, push notifications, and communication history;
- source, responsiveness, appointment, and agent reporting;
- ISA workflows that keep the database clean and move qualified opportunities to agents.

**Ask Magic Mike response:** the existing deterministic scoring, routing, action queue, Lead Center, notification outbox, permission matrix, and advisory Copilot already cover much of this operating model. Phase 9 adds the missing financial and experimental truth layer.

### 4. Predictive seller, homeowner, and database systems

| System | Core mechanism | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Fello | Daily-updated homeowner database, property and behavior signals, AI conversation, warm transfer, outcome tracking | A living first-party database can become a recurring listing engine |
| SmartZip | Territory-level predictive seller scoring, automated marketing, contact data, daily action plan | Market signals are useful only when converted into a daily operator queue |
| Offrs | Predictive seller data, targeted ads, lead feed, ISA/call-center qualification, multi-touch nurture | Separate signal generation, outreach, qualification, and agent handoff |
| Revaluate | Existing-database cleanup and likely-mover scoring with notifications | Rank the database, but expose confidence and never present prediction as fact |
| RealScout / Homebot class | Branded property search, valuation monitoring, market reports, listing alerts, consumer engagement signals | Keep buyers and homeowners in an owned recurring-value loop between transactions |

**Ask Magic Mike response:** build a local intent graph from consented conversations, site behavior, source events, property context, market data, prior outcomes, agent activity, and external signals. Every signal must retain source, observation time, confidence, and evidence.

### 5. Advertising systems

#### Meta

The useful current pattern is Advantage+ lead generation combined with CRM data and Conversions API. Instant forms, website forms, click-to-message, catalog/listing creative, and server-side conversion events can all feed one optimization loop.

#### Google

Local Services Ads can provide call and message leads under a pay-per-lead model. Search and Performance campaigns improve when enhanced conversions for leads and offline outcomes return hashed first-party identifiers and durable conversion IDs within platform windows.

#### TikTok

Native forms, website forms, messaging, logic forms, CRM sync, and Smart+ automation create another acquisition channel, especially for local video and listing narratives.

**Ask Magic Mike response:** platform pixels are not enough. Use server-side, consent-aware conversion events tied to canonical lead IDs and closed outcomes. Never upload test, suppressed, opted-out, or ineligible records.

### 6. Tom Ferry operating system

The repeatable Tom Ferry model is a production discipline rather than a software feature:

- work the database continuously;
- segment relationships and past clients;
- farm a defined geography;
- publish local social and market content;
- use open houses as recurring prospecting events;
- maintain daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannual, and annual touches;
- ask for referrals and provide CMAs or homeowner reviews;
- time-block lead generation, communication, conversion, and appointments;
- inspect execution and conversion every week.

**Ask Magic Mike response:** encode cadence and accountability into the platform. The system should show what deserves attention today, why it matters, what evidence supports it, what draft is ready, who owns the next human action, and what happened afterward.

## Highest-leverage missing layers

### Priority 1: channel economics through close

Before buying more traffic, the platform needs trustworthy cost and revenue truth by source, campaign, geography, intent, lead type, agent, and cohort. Phase 9 installs the data model and read-only command center for this.

Required operating metrics:

- lead volume;
- contactable and qualified rate;
- appointment rate;
- agreement rate;
- close rate;
- spend;
- cost per lead;
- cost per qualified lead;
- cost per appointment;
- cost per signed client;
- cost per close;
- gross attributed revenue;
- referral fees;
- ROAS;
- response time;
- stale lead inventory;
- experiment status and decision.

### Priority 2: vendor-neutral ingestion

Every external source should normalize into the same contract:

- vendor and external IDs;
- occurred and received time;
- minimized contact facts;
- property or listing context;
- first- and last-touch attribution;
- campaign, content, placement, and click IDs;
- explicit consent facts only;
- buyer, seller, renter, investor, referral, open-house, valuation, or listing intent;
- safe metadata;
- payload fingerprint;
- review reasons.

Raw provider payloads should not be retained by default. Signatures, idempotency, replay protection, and provider-specific field maps belong at the edge.

### Priority 3: database reactivation engine

Create cohorts from non-terminal live leads using only operationally relevant facts:

- intent and lead type;
- timeline;
- geography;
- last contact;
- prior engagement;
- property/listing interest;
- prior objections or missing facts;
- communication permission;
- assigned owner;
- market event relevance.

The AI may summarize, rank, and draft. It may not invent consent, send, schedule, reassign, or make property, legal, lending, or valuation claims.

### Priority 4: owned recurring-value loops

Build recurring reasons for consumers to return without a portal:

- personalized listing alerts;
- homeowner equity and market-review prompts;
- neighborhood pages and local market shows;
- school, commute, lifestyle, and relocation guides based on permitted data;
- open-house registration and follow-up;
- seller readiness and home-preparation plans;
- price-reduction and new-listing alerts;
- annual real-estate reviews;
- event and referral programs.

### Priority 5: market opportunity radar

Combine MLS and permitted external data with first-party demand to surface:

- neighborhoods with demand exceeding available inventory;
- price bands with high inquiry-to-appointment conversion;
- stale listings and price reductions relevant to active buyers;
- homeowner segments with repeated valuation or selling questions;
- content gaps visible in search and chat questions;
- farm areas with rising turnover and weak brokerage share;
- underperforming paid campaigns;
- high-performing owned channels worth amplifying;
- agent capacity and routing mismatches.

Each opportunity must include evidence, confidence, score, geography, segment, action class, recommended next step, and review state.

## Build-versus-buy doctrine

### Buy or rent selectively

Use external providers for:

- portal inventory and active demand;
- advertising distribution;
- MLS and licensed property feeds;
- compliant communication transport;
- specialized predictive or homeowner data when economics are proven;
- CRM interoperability when a partner or agent requires it.

### Own permanently

Ask Magic Mike should own:

- canonical lead identity;
- first-party conversation and intent history;
- consent and communication decisions;
- first- and last-touch attribution;
- routing and accountability;
- source and agent economics;
- outcome and revenue truth;
- experiments and decisions;
- local knowledge and brand experience;
- opportunity ranking;
- AI authority boundaries;
- audit history.

A vendor should be replaceable without erasing the brokerage's memory.

## Phase sequence

### Phase 9A: Growth Intelligence foundation

Delivered in this branch:

- additive server-only growth schema;
- marketing channel and campaign registry;
- daily spend ledger;
- lead outcome and revenue ledger;
- experiment registry, assignments, and events;
- market signals, opportunities, and recommendations;
- vendor ingest idempotency ledger;
- deterministic channel-economics engine;
- deterministic opportunity radar;
- vendor-neutral lead-normalization contract;
- protected Growth Command Center;
- tests and release gates;
- no consumer activation.

### Phase 9B: closed-loop integrations

Owner-gated:

1. select initial providers;
2. obtain contracts, API credentials, webhook secrets, and approved field maps;
3. implement signed test-mode ingress for one portal, one CRM, Meta, and Google;
4. reconcile external IDs to canonical leads;
5. import spend daily;
6. send only approved, eligible offline conversion events;
7. verify deduplication and platform reporting.

### Phase 9C: database revival and alerts

1. dormant cohort builder;
2. property and homeowner relevance engine;
3. consent-aware draft generation;
4. operator approval queue;
5. test-record sequence execution;
6. limited consumer pilot only after explicit release language.

### Phase 9D: local market and creative intelligence

1. MLS signal ingestion with licensing and display rules;
2. search and question gap analysis;
3. Wilson-area opportunity heatmaps;
4. neighborhood content and campaign briefs;
5. premium ad, email, page, and social asset generation;
6. experiment and outcome feedback.

### Phase 9E: approved autonomy

Autonomy may expand only by action class and evidence:

- observe;
- calculate;
- recommend;
- draft;
- execute in test mode;
- execute for explicitly approved consumer cohorts;
- execute under caps, stop-loss rules, rollback, and audit.

No capability should jump directly from suggestion to unrestricted production action.

## Definition of competitive victory

Ask Magic Mike wins when:

1. every lead source can be added or removed without changing the canonical operating model;
2. every dollar can be traced from channel to lead, appointment, client, close, and revenue;
3. every live lead has an owner, next action, SLA state, permission state, and reason;
4. the whole database is recycled through useful, consent-aware local value rather than abandoned after a few days;
5. first-party behavior and conversations improve relevance without fabricating facts;
6. controlled experiments replace taste-based debates;
7. local Wilson authority and premium Our Town Properties trust outperform generic national software;
8. AI creates leverage without acquiring unreviewed authority;
9. test and suppressed records never contaminate business reporting or consumer delivery;
10. external platforms become inputs and distribution, not owners of the brokerage's intelligence.
