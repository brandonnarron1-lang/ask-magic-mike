# SMS future activation checklist

1. Owner approves provider, sender, cost, and terms.
2. BIC/legal review approves purpose-specific copy and opt-out handling.
3. Register sender/campaign where required.
4. Store credentials only in the hosting secret manager.
5. Configure webhook signature validation and STOP/HELP handling.
6. Run Brandon-only suppressed QA and verify delivery records.
7. Confirm quiet hours, frequency caps, retries, suppression, and audit.
8. Activate one narrow purpose behind an independent rollback flag.
