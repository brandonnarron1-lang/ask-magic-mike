# Messaging compliance review — Phase 7

Technical guardrails separate requested-service, transactional acknowledgment, appointment coordination, property alerts, marketing nurture, manual one-to-one, internal alerts, and QA. Ambiguous permission fails closed. Phone presence is not SMS consent. Marketing is not inferred from an inquiry. Test/suppressed records cannot receive consumer messages.

Copy avoids appraisal, valuation, offer, availability, appointment, response-time, closing, and outcome guarantees. Fair-housing/protected-trait information is excluded from targeting, scoring, routing, and AI prompts. BIC/legal review remains required before any consumer pilot; this document is an engineering review, not legal advice.
